# Setting up LED Strips in Home Assistant using WLED

*January 31, 2025*

Recently we got bids for a kitchen remodel and some included an option to put
under-cabinet lighting, which I think is kinda cool.  They ballparked it at like
$1,000 and who knows if their tech would even be smart enough to integrate with
Home Assistant which to me is mandatory.  Moreover, that price is pretty high.

Around the same time I heard about the
[WLED Project](https://kno.wled.ge/)
so decided to try it out.

Below are the steps I took to get things basically up and running.  There is a
small bit of soldering involved but nothing fancy.  I'm going to link to the
specific components I used as a courtesy in case helpful to you, but there may
well be better options. I'm not an influencer so I get no kickback from the
sources.  

# Hardware

## Parts

* [D1 Mini](https://www.amazon.com/Hosyond-Wireless-Internet-Development-Compatible/dp/B09SPYY61L/ref=sr_1_4) ($3)
* [WS2812B/SMD5050 individually addressibleLED Strip](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09MKSVV5H) ($11)
* [Low-profile power adapter](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B082QZGY9V) ($7)
* [1' USB-A to MicroUSB cable](https://www.amazon.com/Standard-Charger-Android-Charging-Compatible/dp/B0982S1FHY/ref=sr_1_1_sspa) ($2)(you may need a different length)
* [Self-adhesive LED strip mounting clips](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08KT6BG5F) ($7 for 80)
* [Power Supply](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0D1V32KS1) ($12)

I actually wired up a couple of these using the D1 Mini to power the LED strip, and they certainly work, but
I'm concerned about the power draw from the LED strip (e.g., when it is on bright) burning out the voltage
regulator on the D1 mini.  So after the initial experiment I decided to go with a legit power supply, as
the 114-LED strip can draw up to 8.6A (144 x 60mA) which is way more than the D1 Mini can provide, and in
any case the power adapters I am using are only 3.6A (so yeah, they work but to protect the D1 Mini 
I'd need to only keep them dim).
