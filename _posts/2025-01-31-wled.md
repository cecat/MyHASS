## Setting up LED Strips in Home Assistant using WLED

*January 31, 2025*

Recently we got bids for a kitchen remodel and some included an option to put
under-cabinet lighting, which I think is kinda cool.  They ballparked it at like
$1,000 and who knows if their tech would even be smart enough to integrate with
Home Assistant which to me is mandatory.  Moreover, that price is pretty crazy.

Around the same time I heard about the
[WLED Project](https://kno.wled.ge/)
so decided to try it out.

Below are the steps I took to get things basically up and running.  There is a
small bit of soldering involved but nothing fancy.  I'm going to link to the
specific components I used as a courtesy in case helpful to you, but there may
well be better options. I'm not an influencer so I get no kickback from the
sources.  

Also, the WLED project has very nice
[getting started](https://kno.wled.ge/basics/getting-started/)
instructions, including wiring examples, and tips to avoid
[common mistakes](https://kno.wled.ge/basics/top5_mistakes/).

# Hardware

## Parts

* [D1 Mini](https://www.amazon.com/Hosyond-Wireless-Internet-Development-Compatible/dp/B09SPYY61L/ref=sr_1_4) ($3)
* [WS2812B/SMD5050 individually addressable LED Strip](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09MKSVV5H) ($11)
* [Self-adhesive LED strip mounting clips](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08KT6BG5F) ($7 for 80)
* [Low-profile USB power adapter](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B082QZGY9V?th=1)
* [1' USB-A to micro-USB cables](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0982S1FHY) (you may need a different length; this was just right for the distance from the bottom of my cabinets to the wall outlets)

With the above parts you'd think that driving 144 LEDs (~60mA each ~= 8.6A) would quickly smoke the
power circuitry on the D1 Mini. The default setting for WLED (at least the D1 mini firmware) has a limit
of 850mA ("automatic brightness limiter"), so not to worry.  You can turn this off (if you decide
to go with more powerful supply) via the web interface to your WLED-enabled D1 Mini (i.e., after you complete the **Set up with WLED** instructions below). It's near the top
of the **LED Preferences** page.

# Set up with WLED

The following steps will get your D1 Mini set up with WLED. 

1. Go to the *Assets* section of the
[WLED Github repo](https://github.com/Aircoookie/WLED/releases)
and download the firmware for the D1 mini (I used
WLED\_0.15.0\_ESP8266.bin).

2. Plug your D1 mini into a USB port on your laptop or server.

3. Open a web browser to [web.esphome.io](https://web.esphome.io)

4. Hit **Connect** and Select the USB port your D1 Mini is plugged into.

5. Select **Install** and point it to the firmware you downloaded.  This will erase the D1 Mini
and install the WLED firmware.

6. Your D1 Mini will now act as a WiFi access point and will start to broadcast its WiFi network
(with an SSID  something like WLED AP etc.).  Connect to it (.e.g., from a smartphone if you don't want
to disconnect your laptop from the Internet) and then open a browser (on the same device connected
 to the WLED WiFi network) and you should be taken to the D1 Mini's config page. If not, try going to
https://4.3.2.1.  If you are asked for a password, the WLED default is *wled1234*.

7. At the D1 Mini's WLED page, go to the Info page (an "I" icon) and make a note of the Mac address
as this will help you determine its IP address, which you'll need later for integrating the device
with Home Assistant.

8. Still at the D1 Mini WLED page,  go to *WiFi Settings* and hit *scan* for WiFi networks.
Select yours and enter your WiFi password.

9. Hit save and restart.  The D1 Mini will reboot and attach to your WiFi network (unless you mistyped
the password).

# Integrate with Home Assistant

This part is the easiest.  First, install the WLED integration.  Once you install it,
select it and on the WLED page under *Devices* select **ADD DEVICE** and fill in the
IP address of your D1 Mini. (check your router client list - here is where knowing the MAC
address can be helpful).

The WLED project has good instructions to help with
[Home Assistant integration](https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/wled/).
